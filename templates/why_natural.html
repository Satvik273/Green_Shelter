Natural vs. Conventional Construction

Feature
Natural Construction (GauBaanSa)
Conventional RCC Construction
Environmental Impact
Carbon Negative. Uses renewable resources, sequesters CO2.
High Carbon Footprint. Cement production is a major CO2 emitter.
Cost
More pocket-friendly due to use of local, abundant materials.
Higher cost due to energy-intensive materials like steel and cement.
Health & Comfort
Natural thermal insulation (7-11°C difference), non-toxic, pollutant-absorbing.
Poor thermal insulation leading to higher energy bills. Risk of "sick building syndrome".
Safety & Durability
Earthquake, fire, and pest resistant. Designed for longevity.
Prone to cracking and degradation over time. Requires significant reinforcement for seismic safety.
Waste Generation
Minimal and biodegradable waste. Promotes a circular economy.
Generates millions of tonnes of non-biodegradable C&D waste.
